## Missing signals and future method conditions

**Cold:** preserve navigation start to driver-observed cards/CSS/fonts plus two frame opportunities, including the shared HA shell, interception and the approved 1000 ms maximum per workload. Preserve both historical failures (1108.60 and 1104.80 ms), all valid slow observations and the matched empty/native/experimental one/ten-group cold/warm schedule. The [diagnosis](../../../../experiments/minimum-card-contract/performance-diagnostics/diagnosis.md#readiness) found an empty cold control at 1272.7 ms, with detected mount at 839.7 ms and 425.4 ms from independent second frame to reference observation. Experimental cold mount-to-settlement was at most 3.2 ms. These observations prioritize shared pre-mount startup and post-frame observer delay without attributing them to HA, host scheduling or card code individually.

A separately proposed follow-up must specify finer shared-shell/renderer, module evaluation/registration/construction and driver timestamps that can discriminate these sources. Randomized paired observer/no-observer controls must quantify instrumentation overhead. Keep the original end-to-end reference alongside added phase observations; do not replace it with a shorter phase or subtract the empty control to manufacture a pass. Only test an optimization after a reproducible contribution is isolated. Retain binding/editor round trips, selective updates, single dispatch, layout/theme/reconnect and keyboard checks when comparing an intervention. A new serial, frozen approval-backed verification with comparable controls is needed; repeated lower samples alone do not resolve the historical failure. This review neither changes the ceiling nor claims repeatability.

**Retention:** the [retained-byte diagnosis](../../../../experiments/minimum-card-contract/performance-diagnostics/diagnosis.md#retained-bytes-r2) records passing retained/released, debugger-root, harness and shared-owner controls but failing conditional WeakMap ownership in both default and exposeInternals captures. Independent strong-edge traversal loses ephemeron semantics. Require known-size conditional ownership, shared paths, debugger-group release, harness-only roots and browser/embedder ownership controls, with forced-GC checkpoints and retainer evidence, before agreeing any byte statistic or ceiling. Only then scope the fresh-instance fifty-cycle series (three series/workload, checkpoints every ten cycles). WeakRef collection and zero owned listeners/resources do not establish attributed retained bytes.

**Presentation:** the [presentation diagnosis](../../../../experiments/minimum-card-contract/performance-diagnostics/diagnosis.md#presentation-r3) found clock anchors and changed screenshot content, but no common display-frame identity and successful presentation timestamp; damage occurred in both changed and negative controls. A valid replacement must join the content-bearing frame to feedback for that same frame, test clock mapping to the browser start timestamp, discriminate delayed-change/no-change controls and measure synchronous/asynchronous observer overhead with matched pairs. Use an environment providing those signals if this pinned lab cannot. Calibrate before agreeing latency limits or collecting the thirty-pair-per-workload series. Screenshot capture, MutationObserver and rAF alone cannot establish actual presentation. New methods, environments and campaigns need separate scope.

### Focused maintainer review

The review questions are:

1. **CPU:** which device and complete dashboard should the limit represent, and what main-thread processing allowance is acceptable separately for cold first use and cached repeat use? The existing reference phone remains selected, but its exact build and timings are unobserved.
2. **Transfer:** what minimum bandwidth and transfer-only wait allowance should apply to cold and warm use, with shared HA shell bytes included and separately described?
3. **Updates:** what sustained relevant and unrelated rates represent desk use, should 20 Hz remain required stress coverage, and what main-thread occupancy allowance is acceptable? Is a separate visible-feedback latency objective required beyond aggregate work?

Agreement ledger as of 2026-11-19: load-cpu — no numerical agreement; load-transfer — no numerical agreement; updates — no numerical agreement. The maintainer answered the review: they cannot supply these numbers, do not want guessed limits, and would accept benchmarks of other component libraries as supporting evidence. Their stated concern is avoiding a component so heavy that it becomes nearly unusable. This establishes a qualitative usability objective and a possible comparative evidence source, not a numerical budget, selected comparator or acceptance pass. All three rows remain pending, with no comparable approval-backed verification. The bounded review is complete; deriving supported proposals is follow-up work rather than a request for the maintainer to invent technical thresholds.

### Comparative benchmark direction

Maintainer input recorded on 2026-11-19 permits using other component-library benchmarks to inform future proposals. No library or relative allowance is selected. A comparative result must still be connected to usable dashboard behavior; matching a slow reference would not by itself satisfy the stated concern.

Proposed follow-up method, not an executed campaign: retain native HA and empty-shell controls, select other libraries or cards with documented versions/licenses and explain their functional equivalence. Measure equivalent composition, visual editor and action/data behavior in the same HA/browser/device environment, with matched cache conditions and card counts. Separate library startup/transfer, shared HA costs and relevant/unrelated update work; include dashboard interaction while updates run so aggregate CPU alone cannot imply usability. Report absolute outcomes, relative differences, spread and limitations. Published third-party results may inform comparator selection, but unlike hardware, workloads or endpoints cannot establish a direct ranking or acceptance budget.

Use that evidence and observed interaction quality to bring a justified limit or regression policy back for explicit agreement, without inventing a percentage now. A separately scoped benchmark change must define comparators, representative workloads, usability observations, protocol and disk safeguards before campaigns or dependency acquisition. The existing approved limits, historical failures, unsupported methods and stage 1 exit remain in effect.

Keep the endpoints distinct throughout that review: local UI intent is pointerdown to intercepted local dispatch; HA transport is command submission/completion; physical response is observed desk motion/feedback. A successful command or memory timestamp does not establish arrival. The latter two endpoints were not measured by these UI campaigns and require their own scoped evidence.
