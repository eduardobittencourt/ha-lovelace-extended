# Design

## Context

See [proposal.md](proposal.md) for motivation and [research.md](research.md) E1–E7 for inspected sources. Design is needed because method validity, fresh-data provenance and acceptance evaluation are material performance risks. This design specifies a bounded investigation; it does not assume that retention or presentation methods will become supported.

The selected subject is `experiments/minimum-card-contract`, not `candidates/fixed-composition-card`. Existing public source listings reconstruct the completed benchmark; older diagnostic tools provide phase, heap and frame probes with known limitations. Old review text requesting numerical agreement is superseded by the standing budget delegation.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:** preserve exact subject/environment identity, separate benchmark verification from causal/method probes, and produce mechanically reviewable cell decisions and a ten-row exit disposition. Use valid negative findings to identify the next necessary scope without inventing passes.

**Non-Goals:** editing runtime/editor/CSS/recorder/existing tool sources or lockfiles; changing HA/browser versions; tuning the subject until it passes; collecting physical/phone data; adopting a renderer or shipping a resource.

## Decisions

### 1. Freeze inputs and use external wrappers

Use the CLI-scaffolded change for planning only. During apply, reconstruct/reuse benchmark scripts in the external research directory, verify them against published hashes and wrap unchanged helpers where new evaluation or instrumentation is required. Before launching any collection, record actual Core/frontend/browser/tool versions, container/image identity, CPU allocation, viewport/theme/locale, resource hashes, network interception and storage observations.

The fresh manifest includes the exact adopted-budget file hash, original protocol reference, subject/helper/wrapper hashes, per-mode ordering, event offsets, endpoints, sample counts, invalidation rules and output ownership. Use exclusive run directories; never overwrite a historical attempt. If environment or subject identity does not match, stop the affected verification and report incompatibility. This is preferable to silently broadening the evidence scope or upgrading the environment (E1/E2/E6).

### 2. Run known-valid verification before optional method series

Follow R1's original load/update/interaction schedule serially. Native and empty controls remain visible even when they exceed historical native-envelope values; experimental acceptance is evaluated against the frozen adopted values, never an inflated maximum from the new controls. Shared cost and native's additional body surface remain disclosed.

An external evaluator maps each complete experimental cell to task, script and transferred-byte ceilings independently. Any valid exceedance fails that metric/cell. Missing required coverage is pending; if other valid values exceed the limit, retain the known failure alongside the coverage gap. Invalid attempts are never discarded, retried silently or counted as valid passes. No averaging hides exceedances. Keep queueing, handler-to-intent and final-presentation endpoints distinct; do not compare a new endpoint to an old approval (E1/E2).

Validate evaluator behavior using compact fixtures for equal-to-limit, over-limit, missing/invalid cells, unexpected subject hashes and mixed failure/coverage gaps. Reproduce the report solely from public sanitized samples and the frozen limits. This tests acceptance integrity rather than mirroring collector implementation.

### 3. Diagnose cold variance without replacing readiness

R2 adds paired diagnostic observers around unchanged navigation/readiness helpers and preserves every original-endpoint value. The initial 120-observation paired cold series separates observer-on/off conditions for the three variants and two counts. Instrument host/shared-shell/pre-mount/post-frame/driver intervals without claiming exact isolated card attribution.

Allow one external observer/scheduling revision only when the initial evidence identifies a specific measurement contribution, then freeze a separate same-size paired series. This bound prevents repeated reruns until favorable results appear. No subject optimization, ceiling revision, unexplained outlier exclusion or subtraction from acceptance samples is allowed. The diagnostic result identifies a reproduced cause and its resolution evidence, or explicitly leaves it unresolved. The original failures stay in the decision record even if a causal resolution is demonstrated (E3/E6).

Alternative rejected: accept a fresh sub-1000 ms run as resolution. That would repeat an already observed pass without answering the historical failure.

### 4. Treat retained bytes as an ownership-calibration problem

Use a new external analyzer/probe; preserve `tools/diagnostic-heap.mjs` as the documented failed baseline. Inspect the exact snapshot's conditional relationships and browser/embedder paths before selecting a candidate model. A fixed-point conditional model is a hypothesis, not an adopted implementation: it must pass R3's full retained/released/shared/debugger/WeakMap/DOM/listener/closure controls. One initial candidate and one evidence-led revision are the full pilot allowance.

Only a calibrated method enables native discovery and ceiling selection. Separate native discovery from subsequent fresh verification; freeze per-count/checkpoint native maximum residual-owned-byte values and the method hash first. Three fifty-cycle series with baseline/ten-cycle checkpoints apply to each required count and variant as specified in R3. Native discovery includes matched empty controls. Keep aggregate heap and owned attribution separate and retain paths for surviving removed instances. A native zero residual is a legitimate measured ceiling, not a reason to invent slack (E1/E4).

Alternative rejected: global heap deltas or collected WeakRefs as a substitute byte measurement. They retain their original diagnostic value but do not pass this metric.

### 5. Require successful-presentation identity before collecting latency

Inspect capabilities in the pinned Chromium revision, then run R4's delayed-change/no-change/clock/overhead controls using a new external probe. Successful calibration must associate changed content with an explicit matching frame identity and successful presentation feedback. A color capture plus adjacent trace timestamp is insufficient. No binary/environment migration is included; unavailable signals are a supported negative research conclusion (E5).

Only then collect native latency discovery, select per-count native maxima, freeze the derived limits and collect the independent verification pairs defined in R4. Include matched empty controls and preserve observer-on/off overhead; do not subtract unexplained observer delay or label capture completion physical screen display. The browser-clock state-change trigger is not the old pointer-action boundary or device arrival.

### 6. Keep compact findings in the repository and detailed evidence local

The maintainer clarified the delivery boundary after execution: detailed captures and one-off measurement tooling are local research evidence, not commit content. The repository keeps only `experiments/minimum-card-contract/performance-verification/review.md`, a compact summary of the pinned method, coverage, per-cell ceilings/maxima/decisions, ten required metric dispositions, limitations, source identities and primary references. A directory-local ignore rule excludes detailed artifacts from ordinary Git staging.

The full original package remains under the external research task's `delivery-evidence/performance-verification/`: protocol/environment/calibration files, all scalar attempts, method controls, source listings, numerical freezes, evaluators, replay inputs, regression and cleanup records. Existing raw heaps/traces remain external/private. Preserve failed and unadmitted attempts; do not discard data or relabel a later favorable sample.

The complete sanitized package was validated and reproduced offline before this delivery-format revision. The compact summary is not claimed to be a self-contained replay dataset. Independent numerical replay requires the preserved bundle or a newly scoped capture; reading the findings and primary sources requires no private devbox or Home Assistant access.

Carry previous bounded passes with their exact subject/scope/date instead of inventing new samples. If available R1 data matches an existing endpoint exactly, any fresh reevaluation must identify that fact. Retain editor performance evidence separately from the functional persistence rerun. Stage 1 can close only when every required row is supported and passed, cold causality is resolved with fresh original-endpoint evidence, and correctness/layout coverage remains applicable. Otherwise the summary names the failure or missing capability and the next narrowly scoped work. The synthetic candidate and stage 2 receive no inferred pass (E6/E7).

## Risks / Trade-offs

- Tight adopted native ceilings may fail shared-shell/script/transfer cells -> keep the frozen limits and publish the failure; optimization needs a later change, not retroactive budget inflation.
- Pinned runtime may not expose sufficient ownership/presentation information -> bounded pilots stop unsupported branches; do not run meaningless full series or ask the maintainer for invented values.
- Traces/snapshots or simultaneous campaigns distort measurements and consume disk -> one live campaign, compact scalar data by default, paired overhead probes and storage guard with the existing 1 GiB reserve.
- Old script assumptions or wrapper approval checks conflict with current policy -> verify identities, use additive external evaluation and preserve historical results rather than editing their provenance.
- Causal correlation may not establish a remedy -> retain unresolved cold status and propose only the evidence-backed follow-up scope.

## Migration Plan

No product deployment. During apply, reconstruct only the disposable isolated lab, verify interception and synthetic fixtures, collect/publish evidence, then stop campaign-owned processes/listeners/sockets on all exits. Preserve raw attempts and unrelated state. Before and after capture, verify subject/helper hashes and rerun recorder/binding/selective-update/reconnect and actual visual save/reload/reopen checks using existing tools. Keep existing layout coverage dated and applicable only to the unchanged subject.

Update the README, experiment guide, roadmap, discovery, decisions and relevant budget-policy links from actual outcomes. Publish any new calibrated native-derived limits as policy under delegated authority before their fresh verification. If a step needs product edits, environment migration or a physical device, report that dependency and leave it outside this change.
