# Proposal

## Why

Stage 1 has measured binding/editor correctness and benchmark evidence, but lacks fresh verification against the now-adopted CPU, transfer and update limits and retains unresolved cold-readiness failures and uncalibrated retention/presentation methods. The maintainer has delegated numerical selection to agents, removing the former subjective-objective and separate-approval prerequisites; the next delivery should test the evidence instead of asking for more numbers.

## What Changes

- Deliver a bounded stage 1 / D2 verification and diagnostic investigation for the unchanged two-slot prototype, using the [standing budget policy](../../../../docs/performance-budgets.md) and exact [adopted per-cell ceilings](../../../../docs/performance-budgets.md).
- Freeze a new verification manifest, reproduce the pinned isolated HA/browser setup and collect fresh native/experimental/empty load, update and matched interaction observations. Evaluate CPU, actual transferred bytes and update-only work without moving the goalposts or relabeling discovery as verification.
- Diagnose shared pre-mount and post-frame cold variance with bounded calibrated observer/control runs. Preserve the 1000 ms cold ceiling and both historical failures; publish a causal disposition and reproducibility result, or a precise unresolved blocker. Do not optimize or alter the subject in this change.
- Investigate a corrected retained-byte ownership method and a content-to-presentation identity method. Gate full series on positive/negative controls; select any new applicable numerical ceilings from calibrated native comparator evidence under the existing delegation, without asking the maintainer for arbitrary targets or further numerical approval.
- Publish reproducible sanitized evidence and a reconciled ten-row stage 1 decision record, separating passes, exceedances, invalid/missing coverage, unsupported methods and remaining stage 2 gates.

This is research, measurement tooling and documentation, not product behavior delivery. Its investigation may finish with failed or unsupported acceptance rows; stage 1 closes only if every required row actually satisfies its exit. Planning creates no measurements.
