# Proposal

## Why

The current stage 0 gate makes a particular phone's rendered dimensions and numerical performance baseline prerequisites for the first technical experiment. This delays discovering the library's own behavior even though Home Assistant already supplies dashboard sizing contracts; a reference test environment should not become a product device restriction.

## What Changes

- Revise stage 0 readiness and stage 1 entry criteria to allow a separately scoped, measurable experiment once its questions, contracts, safety boundaries and evidence plan are defined.
- Adopt Home Assistant's documented dashboard sizing conventions as the starting point: Sections through `getGridOptions`, Masonry through `getCardSize`, and internal adaptation to the allocated card space. Native visual alignment remains distinct from reliance on private components.
- Treat the previously selected iPhone as one useful validation environment, not a mandatory development host, exclusive supported device, or fixed product viewport.
- Distribute validation by dependency: define methods before the experiment, gather comparable measurements alongside it, and justify numerical budgets before claiming acceptable performance or completing the MVP. Missing measurements remain visible.
- Document the next experiment's bounded brief: two independently bound elements, visual configuration and save/reopen, isolated inner/body actions, selective updates and cleanup, and container-based layout checks. This change delivers the brief, not experimental code.
- Reconcile current guidance across README, roadmap, discovery, decisions, MVP, architecture and OpenSpec documentation. Preserve historical research as evidence of what was known then.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None. This is a research/documentation change; `skip_specs: true` applies. The capability inventory is empty, and no runtime behavior is delivered.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

Roadmap stages 0–1 and their validation guidance change; the desk MVP, future composition vision and active-promotion milestone retain their scope. Applying this change updates documentation and records research, without introducing a card, dependencies, a public configuration format, a supported-device matrix, HA modifications or physical commands.

Evidence comes from current project documents, the archived versioned frontend assessment, and official HA card/editor documentation. Exact experimental widths, capture environment, stack and numerical budgets remain decisions for the later experimental change, before their dependent execution or acceptance. Native conventions do not prove responsive correctness or performance.
