The final-source diagnostic completed its frozen 36 load observations, 36 editor
observations, 24 update-only windows and 120 idle/under-update local activations.
Every scheduled capture was valid, each input reached its expected local journal
once, and every scheduled state update reached the browser observer with its
final HA state verified. Fifty lifecycle cycles and a subsequent single
activation are recorded separately. See the [public samples](evidence/profile-samples.json),
[offline report](evidence/report/report.md) and [capture provenance](evidence/capture-provenance.json).

Performance acceptance remains pending. The candidate module graph is 32,436
decoded bytes, including the static schema module. Every candidate cold sample
is above the historical experiment's 1,000 ms limit, and one-group warm samples
are above its 750 ms limit. Those historical approvals cover another subject and
verification schedule; the new load boundary also waits for the HA launch overlay
to disappear. These cross-scope observations are concerns to retain, not a new
candidate budget evaluation or a claim that the candidate alone caused shared
startup cost. No old pass transfers and no failure is waived.

The diagnostic records the complete five-Tile native composition and empty
shared-shell context, with their actual resources/cache behavior. Native editor
results cover the cover Tile only, and empty input is a driver-only control.
Three load/editor pairs, ten inputs per condition and one ten-second update
window per cell do not establish robust tails, sustained behavior or isolated
component ownership. Additional delivery/journal observers contribute shared
work. HTTP transfer totals exclude WebSocket payload bytes. DOM/animation-frame
opportunities and local descriptors do not establish compositor presentation,
HA transport or physical response.

The [ten-row ledger](evidence/acceptance-ledger.json) preserves the two historical
cold failures, historical bounded runtime/warm/local/editor passes, unapproved
CPU/transfer/update requirements and unsupported retained-byte/presentation
methods. Representative objectives, applicable agreement and final-MVP/device
verification remain required before a production or cost-based stack decision.
The initial valid profile and exact sources remain separate after the whitespace
accessibility correction; neither run's slow samples were replaced or pooled.
